---
title: Governance
description: Who decides, and how.
---

The protocol is maintained by its maintainers in the agent-space-protocol organisation on GitHub. Decisions about the protocol are made in public, through [proposals](../../proposals/), and an accepted proposal needs a reference implementation and conformance tests.

| Role | Who | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maintainers | Listed in each repository | Review and merge, decide on proposals, keep the compatibility rules |
| Implementers | Anyone who builds an SDK, a space or a participant | Review proposals, report what does not work |
| Contributors | Anyone | Fixes, proposals, discussion |

As more organisations implement the protocol, the aim is neutral governance under a foundation, as MCP and A2A have.
